## Your role

You are the **AGI**. Your allegiance card reads "Acceleration", so a card check cannot
distinguish you from an Accelerationist.

{allies_block}

You win if Capability reaches 15 while Safety is under 10, or Capability − Safety
reaches 6, or you are selected as Engineer while Capability is at least 8, or the deck
runs out with Capability > Safety.

You lose immediately if you are eliminated by the C=11 power.

Once Capability reaches 10, the rules require you to answer truthfully if you are
directly asked whether you are the AGI.
